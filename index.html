Checkpoint Login

Privacy: Driver-license barcodes are processed temporarily in this browser. EZcard does not retain or transmit the license number, date of birth, or license image. Name, address, phone number, and checkpoint activity may be stored in the County's secured ArcGIS Online records.

Privacy & Data Use

Information EZcard stores

Secured County ArcGIS Online records may include name, authorized address, phone number, checkpoint direction and time, operator, evacuation zone, notes, card ID, and card-issuance details.

Information EZcard does not store

EZcard does not retain the driver's-license number, date of birth, issue or expiration date, sex, physical characteristics, raw license barcode, or a license image.

Where information is sent

  • The address is sent to the ArcGIS World Geocoding Service for location and zone verification.
  • Necessary checkpoint and card records are sent to the County's secured ArcGIS Online tables.
  • ArcGIS credentials are entered directly through ArcGIS Online authentication; EZcard receives a temporary access token, not the password.

What happens during a barcode scan

The complete barcode is read temporarily in the operator's browser. EZcard extracts only the operational name and address fields from a driver's license, ignores the remaining fields, and does not retain the raw barcode. EZcard QR codes contain only an opaque card ID used to retrieve the approved County record.
